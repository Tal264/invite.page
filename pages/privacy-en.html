Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when using the website and digital invitation service, how that information may be used, and how we take steps to protect it.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how the website is used, information provided by users or guests may include:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Number of guests or attendance details
  • Messages or notes submitted through a form
  • Other information voluntarily provided as part of an RSVP

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to manage invitations and RSVPs, communicate with guests, plan events and provide the requested service.

Information may also be used for maintenance, security, troubleshooting and improving the service.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

The website may rely on third-party providers for hosting, forms, analytics, storage and other technical infrastructure.

Information may be processed or stored through these providers in accordance with the services they provide and their applicable terms and privacy policies.

4. Google Forms and External Services

When RSVP information is collected through Google Forms or another external service, information submitted through the form may be collected and processed according to that service's own privacy policy and terms of use.

Users are encouraged to review the privacy policy of the relevant third-party service.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies for website functionality, preferences, security and improving the user experience.

If analytics tools are used, statistical information about website usage may also be collected in accordance with the configuration and privacy policy of the relevant analytics provider.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable and commonly accepted measures to protect information from unauthorized access, use, alteration or disclosure.

However, no internet-connected system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, manage the event, comply with legal requirements, resolve disputes or protect legal rights.

8. User Rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have certain rights regarding personal information relating to them, including rights to access, correct or request deletion of information, subject to applicable legal limitations.

Requests concerning personal information may be submitted using the contact details provided on the website.

9. Information About Other People

When a user provides information relating to another person, such as an event guest, the user should ensure that doing so is permitted by applicable law and that any required authorization has been obtained.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version published on the website will apply from the date of publication.

11. Contact

For questions or requests concerning this Privacy Policy or personal information, please contact us using the contact details provided on the website.
